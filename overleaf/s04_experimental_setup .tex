\section{Experimental Setup}
\subsection{Dataset}

\subsection{Sampling, Splits, and Features}
A random sample of 50,000 flows (seed 42, 0.83\% of the file) was drawn, and all non-benign labels were merged into one positive class. The sample was split by stratification into 27,510 training, 7,490 validation, and 15,000 test flows (55.0\%, 15.0\%, 30.0\%). The training partition contained 24,351 attack and 3,159 benign flows before SMOTE and 24,351 of each after it (48,702 total); the validation and test partitions keep the natural skew of about 11.5\% benign flows, so the test set holds roughly 1,720 benign flows. The validation partition was created but not used for tuning or early stopping. A leading unnamed column of the source CSV (apparently a saved row index) was not removed and is among the 42 input columns; its implications are discussed in Section~VIII.

\textbf{Environment }Experiments ran in a Jupyter notebook (Python 3.10) using scikit-learn [21], imbalanced-learn [22], XGBoost [16], LightGBM [17], PyTorch [23], and SHAP [19] on a local Windows machine (16\,GB RAM, CPU-only inference). Times below the wall-clock timer resolution ($<0.0001$\,ms/sample) are denoted as \texttt{<res.} in Table~IV. The combined CSV was obtained from the preprocessed Kaggle IoT-23 compilation derived from the Stratosphere Lab release [13].
